# Efficiency Lab v1 claims policy

## Allowed after deterministic v1 gates

- The measured runtime and commit used by the campaign.
- Physical operation counts for the named scenarios.
- Reference-equivalence and safety-gate outcomes.
- Certified lower bounds and their certificate mode.
- Work amplification for eligible candidates.
- Diagnostic results at 100k/1m nodes with diagnostic labels.

## Not established by this lab

- provider billing;
- LLM quality or token reduction;
- production availability;
- real GitHub, PostgreSQL or cloud connector guarantees;
- universal memory replacement;
- a commercial percentage reduction;
- GA readiness.

Every report must carry a machine-readable claim status. A missing or partial
campaign is `NOT_RUN` or `INCONCLUSIVE`, never `PASS`.
